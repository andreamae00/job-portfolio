About Me

Welcome to my artistic journey! I, Andrea Mae Sinsay, am a passionate Filipino artist driven by a deep love for capturing the beauty of our culture and nature through my art. Growing up in Cavite, I have always been inspired by the vibrant colors of our landscapes, the rich tapestry of our traditions, and the resilience of our people.

My artistic style is a blend of modern interpretations infused with Filipino motifs and My artistic style is a blend of modern interpretations infused with Filipino motifs and symbolism. Through my paintings, I strive to evoke emotions, tell stories, and celebrate the essence of Filipino identity. Each brushstroke is a reflection of my heritage and a homage to the diversity that defines us.
